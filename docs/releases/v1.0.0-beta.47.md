# TorrentLinker 1.0.0-beta.47

## Media: films on the tracker

- With a c411 key, Home and Films add two rows, for films only. **Available on the tracker** shows films your library does not have whose best c411 release reaches your ReleaseScore threshold. **Better versions on the tracker** shows films you have with a release scoring at least 5 points more than your file.
- Each library refresh looks up about twenty films in the background, your films first, then the best seeded films of the Catalogue, and keeps the answers for a week. The lookups share c411's hourly budget with your searches and wait when c411 asks to.
- A tracker film's panel shows its best release, its score, size, sources and how long ago it was published. **Add to download queue** queues it like Search does; nothing starts until you send the queue. The release's category needs a Download Folder.
- In Horizon's Home, both rows are categories of its menu.

## Settings

- ReleaseScore has its own section. Its new **Media suggests releases from** field sets the score the tracker rows require (90 by default).

## Installation

- Windows, Linux AppImage, macOS Apple Silicon, browser companion and Docker distributions are provided by this release.
- Restart an existing desktop or server installation after updating. For Docker, use `ghcr.io/vietnoirien/torrentlinker:1.0.0-beta.47`, or update the `beta` tag.

This is a beta release. Series and season packs are not suggested yet: ReleaseScore cannot score a pack without each episode's runtime.
