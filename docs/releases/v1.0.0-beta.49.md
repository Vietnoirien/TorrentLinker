# TorrentLinker 1.0.0-beta.49

This release is built on the maintainer's machine: **Linux AppImage, browser companion and Docker image only**. Windows and macOS users stay on beta.48 until the next build that includes them.

## tr4ker, a second Tracker

- **Settings → Connexions** has one tab per Tracker (c411, tr4ker), plus Indexers, Clients, Companion and Services.
- tr4ker is searched, crawled into its own Catalogue, polled by RSS through its passkey feed, and used for Reseed and Cross-seed in both directions. Its torrents announce to tr4ker with source `tr4ker`.
- **Search** merges both Trackers, with a Tracker badge on each row and a Trackers switch; c411's rows appear while tr4ker is still answering.
- The companion pairs with tr4ker, reads its profile and logs its payouts. **Update the companion to 0.7.3.** The app keeps the companion current in one folder, and Settings shows which version your browser runs.

## Tracker Films in Médias

- **On the tracker** lists every film the Trackers share, one poster per film with its releases grouped, then rows by genre. A background job identifies each film once (poster, synopsis, genres, runtime, age rating); without a TMDb key, c411 fills it.
- The Owner sees the pre-selected release (your ReleaseScore profile), every release, **Download**, **Wrong film** and **Fix the film**. **Better versions on the tracker** lists films you own that have a release at least 5 points better.
- Members ask for a film and never choose the release; Members asking for the same film share one Request. A Viewer on a Shared Screen asks their account holder.

## Playback

- A file the browser or phone cannot play starts converted, **at its own resolution**: only the sound is converted when the picture plays, otherwise the picture becomes H.264. The resolution is never lowered by itself; a Member's quality limit still applies.
- Each browser checks once which pictures, sounds and containers it decodes, and remembers files it failed on, so the next one made the same way starts converted at once.
- A film that started at its own resolution no longer loads the original first, and an error from a replaced stream no longer lowers the quality.
- The Owner can limit a Member's playback quality. Films start at the last quality chosen on the device.

## Médias

- After a restart, a torrent still downloading into a Library Folder no longer makes every film in that folder read as downloading.
- Covers are small cached WebP images, loaded lazily; posters are downloaded during the scan. The home shows the library without waiting on the Tracker.
- Recent, unwatched and rated rows show a series as one card. A film just started reaches Continue watching at once.
- Films downloaded from another Tracker take the facts c411 filed under their French title.

## RSS, Reseed and Torrents

- RSS subscriptions choose a Tracker, filter by TMDb genre (Required or Excluded), count unread items in the sidebar, and show each torrent's state per client with client actions.
- Reseed shows active items and batch progress, sorts items needing attention first, explains magnet-only candidates and ignored files, recovers a missing qBittorrent location from verified files, reuses a torrent already seeding at the prepared location, and resumes after a restart.
- Torrents selects every match and scrolls instead of paging; a category includes its sub-categories; actions on thousands of torrents are accepted.

## Interface and speed

- One scroll owner per page; each page loads its frame on first visit; static files are revalidated and compressed.
- Library listings share their rows and walk the client's torrents once per page, not once per row.
- A proxy's HTML error page shows as a readable error. A password manager keeps the Owner's password apart from the main domain's.

## Installation

- Restart an existing desktop or server installation after updating. For Docker, use `ghcr.io/vietnoirien/torrentlinker:1.0.0-beta.49`, or update the `beta` tag.
- Update the browser companion (0.7.3).
