TorrentLinker 0.5.3 adds Library batch actions, an archive preflight, and persistent exclusions for unavailable torrents.

## Library actions

- **Check all Candidates** checks previously found, untested Candidates across the Library, including items hidden by filters. It keeps completed checks and continues after individual failures.
- **Reseed all Full Matches** adds confirmed Full Matches through the existing hash Verification flow. Torrents already in the client are skipped, and duplicate hashes are sent once per batch.
- Both actions run in the background with progress and **Cancel batch**. Errors are collected in a single report.
- The new buttons have their own row above the Library list, with French translations.

## Archives

Before a Reseed batch starts, Full Matches inside archives appear in a preflight with their unpacked sizes. Choose **Unpack and reseed selected…** to review an archive's destination, required space and free space before confirming extraction. Or choose **Reseed ready Matches** to send only the files already on disk.

Archives are reviewed individually. The batch does not extract them automatically or overwrite existing files.

## Unavailable torrents

When c411 explicitly reports that a torrent is unavailable for download, TorrentLinker removes it from the local Catalogue and remembers its hash across restarts. Catalogue refreshes and snapshot imports cannot restore it, so future Plans stop proposing it. Authentication errors, rate limits and server errors do not remove torrents.
